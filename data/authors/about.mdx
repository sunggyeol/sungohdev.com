Sung is a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Informatics at **Emory University**, where he works with [Dr. Emily Wall](https://emilywall.github.io/) in the [CAV Lab](https://cav-lab.github.io/). His research spans visualization and HCI, using ideas from cognitive science and AI to understand how people reason and reflect when exploring data visually, and to help them do it better.

He graduated _summa cum laude_ from **Virginia Tech** with a B.S. in Computer Science and a minor in HCI. As an undergraduate, he did research in the [Notification Systems Lab](https://research.cs.vt.edu/ns/) with [Dr. Scott McCrickard](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mccricks/) and the [IDEEAS Lab](https://ideeaslab.com/) with [Dr. Andrew Katz](https://www.linkedin.com/in/andrew-katz-vt/).
