Sung is a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Informatics at Emory University, where he works with [Dr. Emily Wall](https://emilywall.github.io/) in the [Cognition and Visualization Lab](https://cav-lab.github.io/). His research in **visualization** and **human-computer interaction** uses cognitive science approaches and AI to study how people reason and reflect when exploring data visually, and to design interventions that support them.

He graduated summa cum laude from Virginia Tech with a B.S. in Computer Science. During his undergraduate studies, he did research in the [Notification Systems Lab](https://research.cs.vt.edu/ns/) with [Dr. Scott McCrickard](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mccricks/) and the [IDEEAS Lab](https://ideeaslab.com/) with [Dr. Andrew Katz](https://www.linkedin.com/in/andrew-katz-vt/).
