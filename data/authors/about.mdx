Sung is a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Informatics at Emory University, where he works with [Dr. Emily Wall](https://emilywall.github.io/) in the [Cognition and Visualization Lab](https://cav-lab.github.io/). His research in **visualization** and **human-computer interaction** studies how people explore data and make decisions with visual analytics tools, and designs interventions that support them.

He graduated summa cum laude from Virginia Tech with a B.S. in Computer Science. As an undergraduate, he conducted research in the [Notification Systems Lab](https://research.cs.vt.edu/ns/) with [Dr. Scott McCrickard](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mccricks/) and the [IDEEAS Lab](https://ideeaslab.com/) with [Dr. Andrew Katz](https://www.linkedin.com/in/andrew-katz-vt/).
