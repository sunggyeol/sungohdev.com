Sung is a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Informatics at **Emory University**, where he works with [Dr. Emily Wall](https://emilywall.github.io/) in the [CAV Lab](https://cav-lab.github.io/). His research is at the intersection of visualization, cognitive science, artificial intelligence, and human-computer interaction.

He graduated from **Virginia Tech** _summa cum laude_ with a B.S. in Computer Science, a minor in Human-Computer Interaction, and a 3.9/4.0 GPA. As an undergraduate he did research in the [Notification Systems Lab](https://research.cs.vt.edu/ns/) with [Dr. Scott McCrickard](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mccricks/) and in the [IDEEAS Lab](https://ideeaslab.com/) with [Dr. Andrew Katz](https://www.linkedin.com/in/andrew-katz-vt/).
